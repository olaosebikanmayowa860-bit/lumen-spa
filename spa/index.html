About mayowa

Mayowa spa began in 2026 as a two-room treatment space and a shelf of oils we blended ourselves. Every product we sell is one we use in our own treatments: fragrance from distilled botanicals, salts from a single mineral source, and wax poured by hand. We also run massage, facials and sound baths in our studio, and members book first.
